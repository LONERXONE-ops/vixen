## Early observations (hypotheses; 37 scored records)
1. Accessibility scores: 1 in 12, 2 in 6, 3 in 11, 4 in 4, 5 in 4. 24 of 37 records have some primary or secondary text under 3:1 on the conservative measure. Mean final score 70.1.
2. Critical failures (CF1) in 11 records: vixen-ui-017?, vixen-ui-021?, vixen-ui-029, vixen-ui-034?, vixen-ui-036, vixen-ui-037, vixen-ui-038, vixen-ui-042?, vixen-ui-043, vixen-ui-045, vixen-ui-046. Readiness A: vixen-ui-016, vixen-ui-028. Readiness C: vixen-ui-036, vixen-ui-043, vixen-ui-045.
3. Primary labels on brand colors and small secondary text on dark or photo grounds are the most common failures.
4. Content errors (typos, mismatched copy, repeated placeholder data) recur across batches 03 and 04 (025, 037, 038, 040, 044, 045).
5. Missing states (hover, focus, loading, error, favorited) are the most common usability gap.
